OpenPath - Revenue & Business Model

1. Freemium User App

Basic Tier (Free): Users get access to the standard accessibility map and basic routing.

OpenPath+ ($3.99/month): Premium features like real-time voice navigation, offline maps, and instant alerts for broken transit elevators.

2. B2B Partnerships (Business to Business)

Verified Accessible Badges: Local businesses (cafes, restaurants, stores) can pay a small monthly subscription to be "OpenPath Verified." We send someone to audit their accessibility, and they get a highlighted pin on our map, driving more foot traffic to their store.

3. Data Licensing

Selling our anonymized city accessibility data to local governments or city planners so they know exactly which sidewalks and crosswalks need fixing.
