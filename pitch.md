OpenPath: Breaking Down Mobility Barriers 

The Problem: Getting around the city is ridiculously stressful for anyone with a wheelchair, crutches, or just general mobility issues. Standard map apps like Google Maps are great for cars, but they don't tell you if a sidewalk is blocked, if a subway elevator is broken, or if a restaurant actually has a wheelchair ramp. People end up stuck or forced to take massive detours.

The Solution: OpenPath is a community-driven navigation app built specifically for accessibility. It works like Waze, but for sidewalks and buildings. Users can flag broken elevators, missing ramps, or blocked paths in real-time. It calculates the safest, flattest, and most accessible routes so users can actually travel with confidence.

Our Goal: To make the physical world as accessible as the digital one, giving independence back to people who need it most.
